The technology behind it

Every project runs on the same automated data pipeline. It takes your records, applies tested cleaning rules and checks them against live sources. That is why it moves much faster than manual cleanup, at any CRM size.

templates

For the demo, a simple CSV or Excel export. For the project, we work with whatever way of sharing data suits you and your IT team, from regular exports to an API connection.

It depends on the size of your CRM and the scope. After the audit you get a plan with a timeline for each phase, and you see the first cleaned records early on.

Who does the work?

Specialized GTM engineers who work with CRM data every day. We have cleaned and rebuilt large HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics databases for research institutions, consultancies and B2B companies. References are shared on a call.
